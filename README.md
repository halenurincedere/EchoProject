## ❓ What is Echo?

**Echo** is a digital platform where people can safely and anonymously share their emotions without being judged.

Some feelings don’t need to be solved — they just want to be heard.  
Sometimes, a stranger can understand you better than anyone else.

Echo connects users who want to talk with volunteers who are ready to listen.  
No real names. No lasting communication.  
Just presence, empathy, and a voice that echoes within.

---

## 🎯 Why Echo?

- Because everyone needs to be heard sometimes.
- Because sharing without judgment is healing on its own.
- Because listening, voluntarily, is a powerful act of empathy.

Echo is not a "solution."  
It’s simply about being **present** — anonymous, honest, and temporary connection.

---

## 🔄 How Does It Work?

1. The user logs in and selects a mode:
   - “I want to talk”
   - “I want to listen”
2. The system matches two suitable users.
3. A temporary **20-minute chat room** opens.
4. After time expires, the conversation and messages are automatically deleted.
5. If both users agree, they can choose to **match again**.

> **Note:** Echo is designed to be temporary. No messages are stored — but what you feel might remain.

---

## ✨ Core Features

- 🔐 Anonymous, one-time chat
- 🎭 No real names, no profile pictures
- 🕒 Auto-delete after 20 minutes
- ♻️ Optional re-match with same person
- 🪷 **Silent Badge System**: Invisible thank-you notes for listeners
- 📊 Role-based authorization: `User`, `Volunteer`, `Admin`

---

## 🪷 What are Silent Badges?

After every volunteer session, the listener receives a silent "thank you" message from the system. These messages:

- Are private and only visible to the listener
- Are different every time
- Do not affect profile or points
- Serve as gentle reminders of the impact of empathy

**Example messages:**
> “You stayed with the silence today.”  
> “A scream found its echo. With you.”  
> “You made something big, in a small moment.”

## ⚖️ Ethical Note

> **Echo is not a professional mental health service.**  
The goal is to offer a safe space where individuals can share their emotions anonymously and be listened to with empathy.  
Conversations are not saved, and no data is stored.
